Case Summary:
Mr. Chen retained our firm to represent him in a potential wrongful termination and employment discrimination case against his former employer, TechStartup Innovations Inc. He alleges that he was terminated from his position as Chief Technology Officer on January 30, 2024, in violation of California employment laws.

Allegations:
1. Wrongful Termination: Mr. Chen alleges that his termination was without just cause and in violation of his employment contract, which specified that termination could only occur for "gross misconduct" or "material breach" of the employment agreement.

2. Employment Discrimination: Mr. Chen alleges that the termination was motivated by discrimination based on his race (Asian American) and age (38 years old). He states that he was replaced by a younger, non-Asian employee who was less qualified.

3. Retaliation: Mr. Chen alleges that his termination was in retaliation for complaints he made to HR regarding workplace harassment and discriminatory practices.

4. Breach of Contract: Mr. Chen alleges that the company breached his employment contract by failing to provide the required 90-day notice of termination and by failing to pay severance as specified in the contract.

5. Wage and Hour Violations: Mr. Chen alleges that the company failed to pay him for accrued vacation time and failed to provide his final paycheck within 72 hours as required by California law.

Employment Contract Details:
- Employment Agreement dated March 1, 2020
- Position: Chief Technology Officer
