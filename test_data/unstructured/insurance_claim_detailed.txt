Narrative of Incident:
According to the policyholder's statement, Ms. Williams was driving her 2022 Tesla Model 3 eastbound on Wilshire Boulevard in the left lane, traveling at approximately 35-40 miles per hour (the posted speed limit is 35 mph). She states that she had a green light and was proceeding through the intersection at La Cienega Boulevard when another vehicle, described as a dark-colored SUV (make and model unknown), ran a red light while traveling northbound on La Cienega Boulevard and struck the front passenger side of her vehicle.

Ms. Williams reports that she did not see the other vehicle until the moment of impact, as it appeared suddenly from her right side. She states that she attempted to brake but was unable to avoid the collision. The impact caused her vehicle to spin approximately 180 degrees and come to rest facing westbound in the eastbound lanes.

The other vehicle, which Ms. Williams describes as a "dark gray or black SUV, maybe a Ford Explorer or similar," did not stop at the scene. Witnesses report that the vehicle continued northbound on La Cienega Boulevard and was last seen turning east onto Santa Monica Boulevard. The license plate was not clearly visible to any witnesses.

Ms. Williams was wearing her seatbelt at the time of the accident. Her vehicle's airbags did not deploy. She reports that she was able to exit the vehicle under her own power but felt shaken and disoriented immediately following the collision.
